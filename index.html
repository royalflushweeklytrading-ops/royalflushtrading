About Us

RoyalFlush Trading (Pty) Ltd is an Information Technology solutions provider based in Bloemfontein, South Africa. Our team consists of experienced IT specialists and programmers with expertise in software development, systems administration, networking technologies and data solutions.

We support organisations in both the public and private sector by designing, implementing and maintaining modern IT environments. Our goal is to improve productivity, efficiency and security through reliable technology solutions.

We follow structured service delivery processes, clear reporting and preventative maintenance procedures to ensure accountability and dependable service. We are capable of supporting government departments, schools, municipalities, SMMEs and corporate clients under service level agreements (SLAs).
